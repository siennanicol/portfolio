Chicken Curry Recipe

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons oil
  • 1 kilogram chicken (cut into bite-sized pieces)
  • 3 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 medium onion(chopped)
  • 2 inches ginger (minced)
  • 1 piece Knorr Chicken Cube
  • 1 (40-gram) pack Knorr Ginataang Gulay Mix
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 potato (diced)
  • Salt and pepper (to taste)

Instructions

  1. Saute Aromatics: Heat oil in a pot over medium heat. Saute the garlic, onion, and ginger until soft and fragrant.
  2. Brown the Chicken: Add the chicken pieces to the pot. Cook for 5 minutes until lightly browned on all sides.
  3. Add Spices: Stir in the curry powder. Cook for 2 minutes so the spices release their aroma.
  4. Simmer in Coconut Milk: Pour in the coconut milk and add a splash of water if needed. Bring to a gentle simmer. Cover and simmer for 15 minutes.
  5. Add Vegetables: Put in the cubbed potatoes and carrots. Cover and cook for another 10 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
  6. Finish and Serves: Add the red bell pepper and season with fish sauce or salt to taste. Simmer uncovered for 3 minutes until the sauce thickens. Serves hot over white rice.
